Refund requests: my process

  1. Reply within one business day to confirm I received the request.
  2. Check the order against the refund policy and note the reason in the tracker.
  3. Approve or escalate. Clear cases are approved; unusual ones go to the owner with my recommendation.
  4. Tell the customer the amount, the payment method, and when to expect it.
  5. Follow up once the refund shows as processed, then close the case.
